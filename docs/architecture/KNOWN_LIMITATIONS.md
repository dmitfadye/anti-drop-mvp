# Known limitations

1. No real VTB integration, support dispatch, blocking, SMS, compensation or paid cashback.
2. Next.js is preserved separately, not connected to persistent APIs. Verified end-to-end route is HTTP CLI. No frontend merge.
3. Showcase detects a post-event pattern including outgoing funds; prevention of the first transfer is unproven.
4. Thresholds/correlated contributions remain uncalibrated (historical T09). RED is not a legal accusation; low score is not a safety guarantee.
5. Twenty-four handmade fixtures, only two positives, no holdout. Legitimate rent collection still alerts. No production accuracy claim.
6. Seven translation drafts, no native/legal review. Review one priority language before pilot.
7. No interviews, measured adoption, bank-based market reach, causal safe-action lift or bank loss reduction. Economics is scenario arithmetic.
8. Demo identities are not bank IAM. No SLA/load benchmark, public security certification, scheduled retention, restore drill or multi-node reliability proof.
9. Health can be green while PG is unavailable; persistent endpoints then503. Readiness/alerting pending.
10. Legacy cases deliberately410; callers must migrate. Stdlib fallback has no durable workflow. SIM cooldown is educational, not account enforcement.
11. Retention deletes dependent cases/replay keys. Transactional audit is not immutable against administrators.
12. Registration, human team, actual deck/PDF, video/public demo and cloud submission remain unverified/unproduced. Historical audits are not current proof.
