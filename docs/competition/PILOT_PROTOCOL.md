# Proposed pilot — not running

Sponsor hypothesis: bank retail safety/CX owner with security, legal, support and data owners. No agreement exists. Real data/communications require named owners and bank approval.

## Stage 1: synthetic feasibility

12..20 consenting adults, fictional stories, RU plus one native-reviewed language. Compare generic/contextual warnings per PRODUCT_DISCOVERY. Record teach-back, correct action, time, legitimate-task friction and unsafe choices. Proposed gate: severe wording defects fixed; >=80% comprehension per tested language with denominators/intervals disclosed; no critical unsafe instruction observed. Small study is diagnostic, not effectiveness proof. Stop for distress, coerced participation, accidental personal data or misleading bank-action claims.

## Stage 2: read-only shadow

After data/legal/security approval, bank gateway supplies minimal pseudonymous features to private on-prem instance. Verify lineage/time/source and pre/post-transfer arrival. No customer messages, blocking or support dispatch. Versioned decisions and independent reviewed holdout; assess legitimate patterns, timing and alert budget. Bank sets sample size/retention/acceptable FP budget from baseline. Stop on isolation breach, unapproved data, drift, missing audit or exceeded budget.

## Stage 3: controlled warning experience

After stage2 and approved text, small consented cohort in bank app, trusted identity/support routing. Warning-only feature flag; no payment instructions from detector. Preregister comprehension/action/guardrails; measure incremental bank loss only with adequate sample/duration. IAM, TLS gateway, read-only feature feed, support CRM, SIEM and consent are required future integrations.

## Acceptance/rollback/on-prem

Proposed gates: owned access tests, no success on failed commit,16 concurrent same-key attempts produce one case/event, deterministic replay, backup restore drill, agreed latency/load measured at bank volume, no critical pentest findings, scheduled retention/secret rotation, reviewed accessible text. Local tests do not establish SLA.

Rollback owner: bank pilot lead/on-call engineer. Disable UI flag, revoke credentials, stop ingestion, preserve incident evidence under policy, reconcile pending case intents. Existing bank operations remain independent. Prior rule version only after triage. Warning-only module does not alter payments.

On-prem needs approved internal image/package registry, PostgreSQL distribution, SBOM/vulnerability review, private TLS/network, secret store, tenant isolation, metrics and restore. No external inference dependency. Current Compose is local demo, not production infrastructure.
