# Design research — 8 October 2026

Three read-only specialists reviewed architecture, product UX and references. Main agent owns implementation. Direction: airy blue fintech, familiar enterprise navigation, restrained expressive typography. A workspace overview introduces the purpose and clearly states the absence of connected accounts. No dashboard metrics or invented bank data.

## Synthesis

White persistent navigation, pale canvas, editorial two-line heading, explicit prototype badge and a single empty-state surface establish hierarchy. Hand-authored shield SVG is decorative, not a claim of active protection. Native disclosure explains the version's limitations. System fonts preserve legal and offline reliability. No third-party artwork, branding or commercial font files copied.
